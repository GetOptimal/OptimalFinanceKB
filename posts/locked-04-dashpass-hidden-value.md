# The large hidden value of DashPass - and the cheapest way to get it.

If your credit card came with DashPass, you probably activated it and looked at delivery fees. $0 delivery, reduced service fees - fine, call it ten bucks a month. That's the part everyone sees.

The hidden part is a shelf of discounted digital gift cards inside the app. Select brands, 10-15% off, for eligible DashPass members. Nordstrom, H&M, Gap. Not a sale, not a coupon - face-value credit for purchases you already meant to make, bought at a discount before your card earns a single point.

The example: a $100 Nordstrom card at 10% off costs $90. A $200 card at 15% would save $30, but the published $25-per-purchase cap trims it to $25. That cap, plus a four-discounted-cards-per-month limit and one-per-brand rules on some cards, keeps this honest: it's a discount on planned spending, not a money printer. Don't buy $200 of credit to save $25 on a gift you weren't going to give.

The credit card angle is the price of admission. DashPass runs about $120 a year if you pay for it. Chase Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve and Ink Business Preferred include it free - complimentary membership through at least December 31, 2027 depending on activation. That makes the card the cheapest way to get DashPass, and it makes the gift-card shelf pure upside: you paid nothing for the membership unlocking the discount.

The caveats, because this space rotates: the brand list and percentages change - Nordstrom, H&M, Gap and Google Play are on DoorDash's official list as of April 2026. Google Play balance can also pay for YouTube TV when the Google account and payment setup qualify; YouTube TV is not a separately listed gift-card brand here. Total Wine has appeared in short promos but isn't on that dated official list. The offers are account-targeted, so your app is the only source of truth for what's live. Chase's $10 monthly non-restaurant credit excludes gift cards - don't stack it in the math. And on delivery itself, the fees don't fully disappear: it's $0 delivery and reduced service fees at participating merchants.

The hidden value of DashPass isn't free delivery. It's that a membership you got for free can cut 10-15% off retail spending you were about to do anyway. Before your next gift, open the gift-card tab, read what's actually offered in your account, and buy only what you'll use at face value.

## Assumptions

The value comes from discounts on spending you already planned, not from buying credit you might never use.

- **Discount arithmetic:** $100 x 10% = $10 saved, so the price is $90. $200 x 15% = $30 before the stated $25 cap; with that cap the price is $175 and the effective discount is 12.5%.
- **Offer limits:** [DoorDash's Gift Card Plus terms](https://help.doordash.com/en-us/consumers/article/gift-card-plus-benefit-10-or-15-off-select-digital-gift-cards) describe targeted 10%/15% offers, a $25-per-purchase cap, up to four discounted cards per month and separate limits for some brands. The brand list is dated April 8, 2026. Your actual checkout sets today's eligibility, discount, cap and available brand; a special promotion can have different terms.
- **Membership:** About $120 a year or $10 a month is the article's illustrative membership cost, not a fresh subscription quote. [Chase's partnership announcement](https://media.chase.com/news/chase-doordash-benefits) describes eligible complimentary membership and activation limits; card-specific terms control duration. "Free" means no extra DashPass charge on an eligible card you already hold, not a free credit card or proof acquiring a card is the cheapest route.
- **Net value:** The examples assume face-value use of the full gift card, no abandoned balance, no higher merchant price, no extra spending and no purchase fee. They count no reward points and no $10 non-restaurant credit on the gift-card purchase. Delivery benefits require eligible merchants/orders and do not remove every fee. Google Play use for YouTube TV requires a compatible account/payment setup; it is not a separate YouTube TV gift-card offer.
